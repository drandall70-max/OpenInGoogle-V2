Troubleshooting

Missing credentials.json:
Use Start Menu > OpenInGoogle > Setup Google Credentials.

Double-click does not open in Google:
Windows 11 may require manual default app confirmation:
Right-click file > Open with > Choose another app > OpenInGoogle > Always use this app.

Open in Microsoft Office:
Right-click file > Open with > Microsoft Word, Excel, or PowerPoint.

Uninstall:
Settings > Apps > Installed apps > OpenInGoogle > Uninstall.
